Medicine Soup

Interactive story support · English · Primary

How to use this activity: Look at the original comic. Read one section at a time. Choose an answer from each menu, then press Check answers. You can use this file offline in a web browser.

1. Vocabulary bank

Read the word, look at the picture in your comic and say what it means.

HUNGRY
I want food.
HEADACHE
My head hurts.
COUGH
I cough.
TEMPERATURE / FEVER
My body is too hot.
STONE
A small, hard rock.
POT
We cook food in it.
VEGETABLES
Onions, carrots, peas…
SOUP
A hot food we eat.
DOCTOR
A person who helps sick people.

2. Follow the story

Choose the best answer for each question.

3. Put the story in order

Choose the event that comes at each step. Use the comic to help you.
