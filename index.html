🥣 Medicine Soup

Interactive story support · English · Primary

How to use this activity: Look at the original comic. Read one section at a time. Choose an answer from each menu, then press Check answers. You can use this file offline in a web browser.

1. Vocabulary bank

Read the word, look at the picture in your comic and say what it means.

HUNGRY
I want food.
HEADACHE
My head hurts.
COUGH
I cough.
TEMPERATURE
My body is too hot.
STONE
A small, hard rock.
POT
We cook food in it.
VEGETABLES
Onions, carrots, peas…
SOUP
A hot food we eat.
DOCTOR
A person who helps sick people.

2. Follow the story

Choose the best answer for each question.

3. Put the story in order

Choose the event that comes at each step. Use the comic to help you.

4. Sentence starters

The traveller is…
He has… / She has…
He puts… in…
First… Then… Finally…
I think… because…

5. Think about the story

Literal: What vegetables do the villagers add?

Inferential: Why does the traveller say the soup will taste better with vegetables?

Evaluative: Is sharing important? Why?

Think deeper: Does the stone really cure the villagers?

Remember: The stone is part of the traveller’s clever trick; it is not real medicine. Sharing food and spending time together helps the villagers feel better. People who are ill may need appropriate medical care.

6. Helpful reading tips

  • Read one comic panel at a time.
  • Point to the characters and pictures while reading.
  • Give yourself time to think before answering.
  • You can point, say one word, or use a sentence starter.
  • Ask for an explanation if a word is confusing.
